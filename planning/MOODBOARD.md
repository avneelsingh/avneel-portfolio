# Moodboard — Premium Minimal Dark Developer Portfolio

## Palette
- Canvas: near-black charcoal for the primary background.
- Canvas alt: slightly lighter charcoal for raised surfaces and section bands.
- Ink: soft off-white for primary text.
- Ink soft: neutral silver for supporting copy.
- Accent: restrained lime for key actions, highlights, and small status details.
- Lines: subtle charcoal borders rather than heavy shadows.

## Composition
- Editorial asymmetry, a clear typographic hierarchy, and generous whitespace.
- Strong first-screen introduction with a compact education identifier and two clear calls to action.
- Real project descriptions, technology tags, and verified links instead of invented outcome metrics.
- Thin hairline borders, compact tracked-caps labels, quiet hover states, and simple card surfaces.
- Mobile-first layout, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Fonts
- Display: Fraunces for large editorial headings.
- Body: General Sans with Inter/system fallbacks for readability.

## Motion
- Short, low-distance reveal transitions only when useful.
- No motion dependency for navigation, links, or access to core content.
- Respect the operating system's reduced-motion preference and clean up animation tickers when the app unmounts.
